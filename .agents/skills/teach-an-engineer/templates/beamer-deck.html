The mechanism, drawn

\[ H_{k+1} \;=\; V_k^{\mathsf T} H_k V_k + \rho_k\, \boldsymbol{y}_k\boldsymbol{y}_k^{\mathsf T} \]
\[ V_k \;=\; I-\rho_k\, \boldsymbol{y}_k\boldsymbol{s}_k^{\mathsf T} \]
  • \(\boldsymbol{s}_k=\boldsymbol{x}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{x}_k\), \(\boldsymbol{y}_k=\boldsymbol{g}_{k+1}-\boldsymbol{g}_k\), \(\rho_k = 1/(\boldsymbol{y}_k^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol{s}_k)\)
  • Each factor is a rank-two correction — no matrix is inverted.

Limits, and what to read next

Where it breaks

  • Nonconvex \(f\): \(B_k \succ 0\) can fail — damping repairs it.
  • Nonsmooth \(f\): the secant equation carries no curvature information.
  • Memory: \(\mathcal{O}(m n)\) per iteration; \(n\) dominates.
