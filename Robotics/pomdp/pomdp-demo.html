POMDP belief update: the tiger problem

The agent's knowledge is one number, b = P(tiger is left). Drag the belief, choose which growl arrives, and watch Bayes's rule move it. The state itself never appears.

Opening the safe door pays +10, the tiger door -100, listening -1; a growl is correct with probability 0.85. Numbers follow Kaelbling, Littman & Cassandra (1998), §5.1.
